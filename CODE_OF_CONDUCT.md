# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed CC BY 4.0). The summary below is binding; the linked text is the full reference.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, empathetic and constructive; give and gracefully accept feedback.
- Assume good faith; focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, trolling, sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information (including **real personal images or ID documents**) without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and when representing the project in public).
Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the contact options on the
[@cybersafetyid](https://github.com/cybersafetyid) GitHub profile or a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/cybersafetyid/Faktel/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled promptly and
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
